• What it is: The apex optical uplink terminal for high-bandwidth external telemetry and orbital synchronization.
• Technologies inside: Monolithic optically transparent synthetic sapphire capstone dome, Free-Space Optical Communication (FSOC) laser transceivers, and an internal mirror-based gyroscopic high-precision beam positioning system.
• Purpose: To enable secure, ultra-high-speed terabit data routing between the ASI nucleus and satellite constellations via directed light beams, rendering the communication channel completely immune to radio-frequency jamming, electronic warfare (EW), and EMP events.
