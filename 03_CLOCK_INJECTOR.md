• What it is: The macro-scale acoustic-mechanical clock generator for hardware synchronization.
• Technologies inside: 7-step corbeled acoustic waveguide (inclined hallway configuration), high-purity monocrystalline quartz resonator rods, and piezo-electric frequency injection interfaces.
• Purpose: To capture baseline tectonic micro-vibrations and hydraulic pressures, converting them through compressed acoustic wavefronts and the direct piezo-effect into a rock-solid, unjammable hardware clock frequency to synchronize the ASI tensor arrays.
