• What it is: The sub-surface kinetic energy harvesting foundation and primary cold-start power supply.
• Technologies inside: Sub-terranean hydraulic intake channels, compressed tectonic quartz beds, high-capacity graphene-based supercapacitors (ionistors), and solid-state power conditioning circuits.
• Purpose: To convert continuous underground water flow, hydraulic shocks, and ambient seismic micro-vibrations into a stable electric current, providing the monolith with permanent, grid-independent power for auxiliary systems and the initial core boot-up sequence.
